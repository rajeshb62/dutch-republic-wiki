Dutch Republic

Overview

The Dutch Republic was the maritime, commercial, military, and cultural frame for this wiki: a federation of provinces and cities that emerged from the Revolt against Spain and became the seventeenth-century hub of European shipping, finance, overseas trade, cartography, science, and colonial expansion. This page is a navigation hub tying together the Republic’s political revolt, urban economy, VOC/WIC empire, naval wars, and Golden Age culture. ^[source: prak-dutch-republic-17th-century.txt; de-vries-woude-first-modern-economy.txt]
